Experience design

Cobalt Room

A cinematic editorial presence for an intimate events venue.

Challenge

The brief we walked into.

The room had atmosphere in person and a brochure online. Night photography fought a generic template, and the booking path felt like an inquiry form rather than an invitation.

Approach

How the system came together.

We treated the site as editorial experience design: cinematic pacing, a restrained palette, and copy that sounds like the room. The Flask build stays quiet so the images and type can carry the evening.

Outcome

What launched.

A presence that feels like the venue—intimate, considered, and ready to book—without losing the operational clarity a small team needs.
