Product website

Meridian

A conversion-focused site with clear paths for buyers and partners.

Challenge

The brief we walked into.

Buyers and partners were landing in the same funnel. The product story was strong, but the next step was not, and comparison pages repeated the same pitch instead of routing intent.

Approach

How the system came together.

We rebuilt the information architecture around two audiences, with conversion paths that stay specific: proof for buyers, context for partners, and a contact brief that arrives ready for the next conversation.

Outcome

What launched.

A product website that routes intent without diluting the story, and a visual system that can grow with the catalogue.
