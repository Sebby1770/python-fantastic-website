Studio note

A small practice with a long memory for paper.

Asteria Studio designs and builds websites for people who still believe a page can have a point of view. We work in Flask, write the interface ourselves, and leave a repository you can inherit.

The studio is named for a navigational star, not a constellation of services. We take on a handful of sites a year: brand platforms, product narratives, editorial catalogs, the occasional venue that deserves better than a Facebook page. If the work is a dashboard that must feel like one, we will say so and point you elsewhere.

What we will do is sit with the offer until it can be said in a sentence, then build the system around that sentence. Visual direction is not a layer we pour on at the end. It is how the information stands up.
